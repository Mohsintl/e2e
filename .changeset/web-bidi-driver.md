Experimental WebDriver BiDi driver for chromium and firefox: `web({ driver: 'bidi' })`, or `E2E_WEB_DRIVER=bidi` to switch a whole run. It drives observation, locators, actions, the keyboard, navigation, screenshots, saved sessions, and the `browser` fixture's navigation, evaluate, cookie, viewport, keyboard, and mouse calls. Request routing, `waitForResponse`, dialogs, downloads, media emulation, frames, video, `connect`, browser providers, and the header, basic-auth, and emulation options refuse with an error naming the gap. Playwright stays the default.
